### Business Context

Sales representatives need a consistent way to record each Contact's preferred communication channel. Recording this directly on the Contact helps representatives reach people in the way they prefer and supports the goal of getting more replies. The change adds an optional choice among Email, Phone, and WhatsApp.

### Scope

* In scope:
    * Add an optional restricted Contact picklist with Email, Phone, and WhatsApp.
    * Grant intended sales representatives field read/edit access through a permission set.
    * Display the field on the Contact layout used by sales representatives.
* Out of scope:
    * Sending messages, integrations, consent tracking, automation, or defaulting the field.
    * Changing Contact sharing or profile permissions.

### Acceptance Criteria

* AC1: Given a sales representative can edit a Contact, when they choose Email, Phone, or WhatsApp and save, then the selected value is saved and only those three choices are offered.
* AC2: Given a sales representative can edit a Contact, when they leave Preferred Contact Method blank and save, then the Contact saves successfully with the field blank.
* AC3: Given a sales representative views a Contact using the layout assigned to sales users, when they open the Contact page, then Preferred Contact Method is visible.

### Technical Notes

Add a restricted custom picklist, provide field-level access through a dedicated permission set, and add the field to the Contact layout assigned to sales reps. Standard Contact sharing and CRUD remain unchanged; Salesforce UI enforces CRUD/FLS, and no Apex, Flow, or integration is required.
