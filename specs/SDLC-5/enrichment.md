### Business Context

Sales representatives need to track contract end dates on customer Accounts and identify upcoming renewals. A current, visible warning helps them begin renewal conversations before a contract lapses. Other intended Account viewers should be able to see the date and warning without editing the date.

### Scope

* In scope:
    * Add an Account contract end date and formula-driven renewal-due indicator.
    * Display both fields on the Account layout or layouts assigned to intended users.
    * Grant date edit access to authorized sales users and read-only access to intended viewers through permission sets.
* Out of scope:
    * Renewal emails, tasks, notifications, opportunities, or other lifecycle automation.
    * Changes to Account CRUD, record sharing, profiles, or unrelated contract processes.

### Acceptance Criteria

* AC1: Given a sales representative with Account edit access and the editor permission set, when they enter or change a contract end date and save, then the date is stored and editable.
* AC2: Given an Account end date from today through 30 days from today inclusive, when an intended viewer opens the Account, then the renewal-due indicator is true.
* AC3: Given an Account end date that is blank, in the past, or more than 30 days away, when an intended viewer opens the Account, then the renewal-due indicator is false.
* AC4: Given an intended viewer without the editor permission set and an authorized editor, when each views the Account and attempts to edit the date, then the viewer can read both fields but cannot edit the date and the authorized editor can edit it.

### Technical Notes

Use an optional Account Date field and a formula Checkbox that is true from today through day 30 inclusive. Display both on the Account layout(s) already assigned to the intended users; grant field access with least-privilege editor and viewer permission sets. No Apex or Flow is required.
